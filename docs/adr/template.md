# NNNN. Short imperative title

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Deciders:** who owns this decision

## Context

What is the problem, constraint, or force that makes a decision necessary?
Two or three sentences, including the alternatives considered and why they are
not obviously sufficient.

## Decision

What we will do, stated in the active voice: "We will ...". Include the concrete
mechanism, not only the intent.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. Name the ceilings, the follow-up
work, and the conditions under which this decision should be revisited.
